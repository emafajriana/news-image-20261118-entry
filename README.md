# Wednesday 18 November 2026: 40 stories, 6 continents, one front page and a map

Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261118** ("Sum up 18 November's
world news in one image").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the bundled picture
against the competition limits (whole PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB, at most
1024x1024) and copies it byte for byte to `entry.png` in the output
directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set, otherwise `/output`). The written
file is read back and its sha256 compared, so a bad write fails loudly.

The picture: a newspaper front page for **Wednesday 18 November 2026** —
the date itself is the masthead — carrying **40 stories across 6
continents**:

- **3 hero panels:** US sinks five Iranian tankers (Iran hits 10 ships and
  a US base in Jordan; oil nears $100); the Kerch Bridge arch falls into
  the sea; the Dow drops 849, briefly down 1,000, before Nvidia beats
  after the bell.
- **8 small illustrated panels** with flag chips: Trump lands in Manila
  for Quad talks; the UN warns of deaths from the cold in Gaza; COP31
  climate talks hit their decisive phase; North Korea fires a ballistic
  missile; Herzog taps Eisenkot to form Israel's next government; Houthis
  hit four Saudi cities; Cyclone Akvan churns in the Bay of Bengal; a
  Russian military plane crashes in Siberia, killing 19.
- **Map and index:** a world map with numbered, colour-coded pins for
  the 11 pictured stories, beside a numbered index of all 40. The other
  29 include US jets striking a missile site on Qeshm Island, the
  suspected Iran dirty-bomb plot in Washington, an Iranian agent jailed
  for 27 years over a Trump murder plot, Iran's Hormuz restricted zone,
  Kuwaiti and Bahraini air defences downing drones, the US scolding Kyiv
  over deep strikes, Pattinson named the new James Bond, UK PM Burnham
  floating closer EU ties, Britain sanctioning settlement firms,
  France's 4th weekend of protests, a freezer cyber-attack in 7 nations,
  DR Congo peace efforts failing, $12bn in Venezuelan oil revenue,
  Canada–China trade deals, China accused over Taiwan voters, Pakistan's
  petrol cut, an NSA network outage, Anthropic's planned IPO,
  Salesforce's 9.5% fall, the Smithsonian chief stepping down, the new
  Knesset sworn in, quakes in Indonesia and Papua New Guinea, India's
  10th SAFF title, Pakistan edging India in blind cricket, Dhoni's IPL
  retirement, Jay Shah's ICC re-election and World COPD Day.

All illustrations (panels and map) are original, generated for this
entry; all text is rendered programmatically. Sources: CENTCOM,
Reuters, Al Jazeera, AP, BBC, NPR, GFZ, Politico — as reported on
18 November 2026.
